### Smew

The smew is a small, compact diving duck that breeds around northern lakes and slow-flowing rivers.

### Black-throated loon

The black-throated loon is built for diving and breeds on clear freshwater lakes.

### Common goldeneye

The common goldeneye is a cavity-nesting diving duck with a fast, whistling wingbeat.

### Common merganser

The common merganser is a large fish-eating duck with a long, narrow, serrated bill.

### Red-breasted merganser

The red-breasted merganser is a slender diving duck found on both coastal and inland waters.
