### European green woodpecker

The European green woodpecker often feeds on the ground, using its long tongue to collect ants.

### Black woodpecker

The black woodpecker is Europe's largest woodpecker and excavates large nest holes in mature trees.


### Great spotted woodpecker

The great spotted woodpecker is a widespread, adaptable species with bold black, white and red plumage.


### Lesser spotted woodpecker

The lesser spotted woodpecker is Europe's smallest woodpecker and often forages on thin branches.


### Eurasian wryneck

The Eurasian wryneck is an unusual migratory woodpecker with cryptic plumage and a twisting defensive display.
