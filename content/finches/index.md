### Eurasian bullfinch

The Eurasian bullfinch is a sturdy finch with a short, powerful bill for feeding on buds and seeds.

### European greenfinch

The European greenfinch is a stocky seed-eater, identified by the yellow flashes in its wings and tail.

### Brambling

The brambling breeds in northern forests and often gathers in flocks during migration and winter.

### Pine grosbeak

The pine grosbeak is a large, calm finch of coniferous and birch forests.
