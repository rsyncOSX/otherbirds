### Common tern

The common tern is a graceful migrant that catches small fish by plunge-diving.

### Black-headed gull

The black-headed gull is a familiar wetland and coastal bird; adults develop a dark brown hood in summer.

### Eurasian oystercatcher

The Eurasian oystercatcher uses its strong orange-red bill to feed on shellfish and other shore animals.
