Despite the gallery title, only the Eurasian blue tit belongs to the true tit family, Paridae. The long-tailed tit, Eurasian nuthatch and yellowhammer each belong to a different family.

### Long-tailed tit

The long-tailed tit is a tiny, sociable bird with a tail longer than its body.

### Eurasian blue tit

The Eurasian blue tit is an agile woodland bird with a blue crown, wings and tail.

### Eurasian nuthatch

The Eurasian nuthatch moves head-first down tree trunks while searching the bark for food.

### Yellowhammer

The yellowhammer is a bunting of open countryside, recognised by the male's bright yellow head.
