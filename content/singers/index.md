### Willow warbler

The willow warbler is a widespread summer visitor whose soft song is a familiar sound in Norwegian woodland.

### Wood warbler

The wood warbler favours mature deciduous woodland and has a distinctive accelerating trill.

### Eurasian blackcap

The Eurasian blackcap is a rich-voiced warbler; the male has a black cap and the female a chestnut-brown one.
