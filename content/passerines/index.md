### Goldcrest

The tiny goldcrest is identified by the bright crown stripe that gives the species its name.


### Eurasian treecreeper

The Eurasian treecreeper spirals up trunks while probing bark for small invertebrates.

### Common redpoll

The common redpoll is a small finch with a red forehead and a fondness for birch and alder seeds.


### Common reed bunting

The common reed bunting is strongly associated with wetlands, reedbeds and damp scrub.


### Common whitethroat

The common whitethroat is a summer visitor that nests in low, dense vegetation.

### Eurasian skylark

The Eurasian skylark is famous for its sustained song flight over open farmland and grassland.

### Eurasian wren

The Eurasian wren is a very small bird with a powerful voice and a characteristically cocked tail.

### Bohemian waxwing

The Bohemian waxwing is a sociable winter visitor that feeds on berries and fruit.
