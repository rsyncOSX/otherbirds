All four species belong to the wagtail and pipit family, Motacillidae. The citrine wagtail is a [rare but regular visitor to Norway](https://www.birdlife.no/organisasjonen/nyheter/?id=1213) and was first recorded in the country near Tønsberg in 1970.

### Citrine wagtail

The citrine wagtail is an eastern species whose breeding male has a bright yellow head and underparts.

### Western yellow wagtail

The western yellow wagtail favours open, damp ground and is highly variable in head pattern and colour.

### White wagtail

The white wagtail is a familiar black, white and grey bird that constantly pumps its long tail.

### Grey wagtail

The grey wagtail is closely associated with fast-flowing water and has a notably long tail and yellow underparts.
