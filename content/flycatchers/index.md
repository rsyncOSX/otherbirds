### Common redstart

The male common redstart has a bright orange-red tail and underparts with a contrasting dark face.


### Bluethroat

The bluethroat is a small migratory bird whose male has a striking blue throat in breeding plumage.



### European robin

The European robin is familiar by its orange-red face and breast and its clear, delicate song.


### European pied flycatcher

The European pied flycatcher nests in tree holes and nest boxes and catches insects in short aerial flights.



### European stonechat

The European stonechat often perches upright on exposed stems while watching for insects.

### Spotted flycatcher

The spotted flycatcher usually hunts from a favourite perch, flying out to catch insects in the air.
