The horned grebe is listed as [vulnerable in Norway](https://lister.artsdatabanken.no/rodlisteforarter/2021/28011) and is, in my opinion, one of the country's most beautiful birds. Both species belong to the grebe family, Podicipedidae.

<!--more-->

| Scientific name | English | Norwegian | Family |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Podiceps cristatus | [Great crested grebe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_crested_grebe) | [Toppdykker](https://no.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toppdykker) | Podicipedidae |
| Podiceps auritus | [Horned grebe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horned_grebe) | [Horndykker](https://no.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horndykker) | Podicipedidae |

### Horned grebe

In breeding plumage, the horned grebe has golden ear tufts, a dark head and a rich chestnut neck.


### Great crested grebe

The great crested grebe is known for its ornate head plumes and elaborate courtship display.
