### Roe deer

The roe deer is a small, agile deer commonly found along woodland edges and in farmland.



### Red squirrel

The red squirrel is an agile tree-dweller that feeds mainly on seeds, cones, fungi and buds.

### Moose

The moose is the largest member of the deer family and is well adapted to forests and wetlands.


### Mountain hare

The mountain hare grows a paler winter coat that provides camouflage in snowy landscapes.

### Red fox

The adaptable red fox lives in habitats ranging from coast and farmland to forest and mountain areas.

### Red deer

The red deer is one of Norway's largest land mammals and is especially common in western regions.
